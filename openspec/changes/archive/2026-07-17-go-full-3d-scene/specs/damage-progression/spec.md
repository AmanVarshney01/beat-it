# damage-progression

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Noodle mess marks

The system SHALL paint a sauce stain at noodle impact points (located like other marks, capped, reset-cleared) and SHALL attach draped noodle strand meshes to the head (capped count, cleared on reset).

#### Scenario: Stain and strands

- **WHEN** noodles land on the face
- **THEN** a sauce stain appears at the impact point and strands drape on the head until reset
